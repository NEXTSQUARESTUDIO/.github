# Support

For game support or business enquiries, contact **[contact@nextsquarestudio.com](mailto:contact@nextsquarestudio.com)** or visit **[nextsquarestudio.com](https://www.nextsquarestudio.com/)**.

For a problem in a public GitHub repository, use that repository's bug report template.

Please include:

- The game, app, or repository name.
- The version and device or operating system.
- What happened and what you expected.
- Steps to reproduce the problem and a screenshot if useful.

Do not post passwords, purchase receipts, account details, or other private information in a public issue.
